---
title: Hiding the Blog Page
description: Sometimes people may want to hide the blog page of their site. Now you can!
date: 2026-02-16
---

Why would someone want to hide the Blog page...on a blog? Well, sometimes people do. For example, on this site I have hidden the blog page as it's not needed. While there is a "blog" as such, it's use for [docs](/docs) and I want to have finer control on how the posts are listed there.

So this is a perfect use case for hiding a blog. Or, maybe someone just wants to use Pure Blog as a site that has pages, but no blog. Now they can!

To hide your blog page, head to `Settings` then `Site Settings`, scroll down to the `Blog Page` option and set it to *"Hidden (disable blog page)"* and your blog page will be no more.

<p class="notice">If you have any published blog posts, they will still be available via their URL. The RSS feed will remain available too.</p>
